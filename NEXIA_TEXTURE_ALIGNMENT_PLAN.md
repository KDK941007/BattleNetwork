### B-2. Solve position alignment (critical path)

- [ ] Identify correspondences between illustration and model **for each body part**. Start with HEAD/FACE, then TORSO, ARM-L/R, LEG-L/R, FEET-L/R.
- [ ] Record 2D anchors per image and matching 3D points. For HEAD, include top center, ear centers, visor ends, mask tip, eyes; for TORSO, chest emblem and armor edges; for arms/legs, joints and major armor ridges.
- [ ] Compute/author separate part-specific UV or projector transforms from landmarks rather than one full-body world-space projection. Where model proportions differ, correct mesh or texture UVs as a *separate conscious decision*—not silent deformation.
- [ ] Keep original image colors; avoid re-interpreting shapes based solely on artificial rectangular eye masks.
- [ ] Avoid final normal-based blending until seam placement and anchor positioning are sound.

Exit: Head anchors line up front AND right (and back/left where meaningful). No obvious painted eyes floating outside the geometry.

### B-3. Head-only pilot (strictly bounded)

- [ ] Produce ONE head-only material alignment output on a protected model copy.
- [ ] Review four orthographic views (plus one perspective view), first with references behind then with references hidden.
- [ ] Permit at most ONE targeted correction cycle for this head pilot. If the result still has large feature offsets or obvious projected facial features on wrong surfaces, **stop this procedural approach** and explicitly choose manual UV/retopology or purpose-built head remodelling. Do not repeat screenshot/debug loops.
- [ ] Do not claim success unless eyes, mask boundary, forehead and ear details visually align in both front and side, without obvious projections across unintended faces.

### B-4. Extend to rest of body

- [ ] Use proven alignment method for TORSO, left/right ARMS, left/right LEGS and BOOTS; review front/side/back/left.
- [ ] Correct significant shape mismatches separately. Explicitly track which ones cannot be solved with texture.
- [ ] Bake approved projections to standard UV texture image(s); do not depend on the temporary multi-view procedural shader as the eventual game asset.

### B-5. Game asset readiness (separate milestone)

- [ ] Review topology/decimation or retopology, rigging, skin weighting and game-export requirements.
- [ ] Verify texture preservation and animation in glTF/GLB; do not claim the original 749k-vertex model is already performance suitable.

## Definition of done

1. Four approved illustrations with independent left view.
2. Painted feature locations match the corresponding 3D surfaces from all four orthographic views, including consistent eye and ear locations.
3. No prominent seam smearing or reference-label/background contamination.
4. The final model and texture remain editable, original assets preserved, and export verified.
5. Separate acceptance for game optimization and animation; do not conflate a good viewport preview with a production-ready asset.
